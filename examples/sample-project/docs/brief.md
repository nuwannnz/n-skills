# PawPlan Brief

## Idea

PawPlan lets pet owners book vet appointments online at a single small clinic,
instead of phoning during opening hours. Clinic staff manage the vets'
schedules in the same app.

## Problem

Owners can only book by phone between 8:00 and 18:00, and the front desk spends
about three hours a day on booking calls. Missed calls turn into missed
appointments, and staff double-book when two people edit the paper diary at
once.

## Target Users

- Pet owners, who want to book, see, and cancel appointments at any time from
  their phone.
- Front-desk staff, who want fewer phone calls and one schedule they can trust.
- Vets, who want to see their day at a glance.

## Goals

- Move 50% of bookings online within three months of launch.
- Cut front-desk booking calls by 40% in the same period.
- Zero double bookings.

## Constraints

- One clinic, one location, for the first release.
- Must run on the clinic's existing budget of about 50 USD a month for hosting.
- Owner data must stay in the EU.

## Raw Input

> We're a 4-vet clinic and the phone never stops. I want owners to book online
> like they do at the hairdresser. Staff need to block out surgery days and
> holidays. Send people a reminder the day before because no-shows kill us.
> Keep it cheap, we're small. Data has to stay in Europe.
